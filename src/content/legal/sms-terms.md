**Plain-language summary:** If you check the SMS box in your Account settings, {{BRAND}} can text you about your own account and jobs (new leads, new messages, job status, reminders, weather heads-ups, review requests). We do not send third-party marketing texts, and we never share your phone number with other companies for their own marketing. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop, or HELP for help. You can also turn texting off in Account without texting anything.

## 1. The program

Program name: **{{BRAND}} Alerts**.

{{BRAND}} Alerts sends account and job-related text messages tied to your own use of the {{BRAND}} app, including:

- New lead notifications (contractors)
- New message alerts (a reply came in on a job or chat)
- Job status updates (a quote arrived, a job was closed, an application changed)
- Appointment and maintenance reminders
- Weather heads-ups for your area (freeze, heat, wind, and heavy rain forecasts)
- Review requests after a job

{{BRAND}} Alerts is not a marketing list and is not shared with or operated by any third party. We do not send promotional messages on behalf of advertisers, partners, or any other company through this program.

## 2. How you opt in

Texting is off by default. To turn it on, go to Account, enter your phone number, and check the box. For homeowners the box reads:

> "Text me at this number for account and job-related messages (like a reminder to review a pro after a job). Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. This number is never used for marketing from other companies."

For pros, the box shown during pro onboarding reads:

> "Text me when a job matches or a homeowner replies. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. This number is never used for marketing from other companies."

The checkbox is unchecked until you check it. Agreeing to receive texts is never required to create an account, post a job, hire a pro, or make a purchase on {{BRAND}}. You can use {{BRAND}} fully without ever opting in to SMS.

## 3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your account activity. Most weeks you should expect to receive a small number of messages, generally up to a few per week. Reminders and similar nudges are capped at two (2) per rolling seven (7)-day period. Weather heads-ups are not counted in that cap, so a stormy week can bring more. Transactional messages tied to something happening on your own account, such as a new message or a job status change, are not capped by that limit and may arrive more often if your account is active.

## 4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for your plan's rates. {{BRAND}} does not charge you for text messages.

## 5. How to opt out

Reply **STOP**, **STOPALL**, **UNSUBSCRIBE**, **CANCEL**, **END**, **QUIT**, **REVOKE**, or **OPT OUT** to any {{BRAND}} text message at any time to stop all texts from us. You will receive one (1) confirmation message:

> "You have opted out of {{BRAND}} text messages. No further messages will be sent. Reply START to opt back in. For help, reply HELP."

You can also opt out, or back in, any time in Account, by unchecking or checking the SMS box. Opting out of texts does not affect your account, your subscription, or any other part of the {{BRAND}} service. It only stops text messages.

To opt back in, reply **START**, **YES**, or **UNSTOP** to a previous {{BRAND}} number, or re-check the box in Account.

## 6. How to get help

> "{{BRAND}} Alerts: account and job texts. Help: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} or {{BUSINESS_PHONE}}. Msg&data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out."

## 9. Quiet hours

We do not send text messages before 8:00 AM or after 9:00 PM Pacific time. This applies to every text, including alerts about a new message or lead. A text that would fall in those hours is not sent later. If something happens overnight, you will still see it in the app.
