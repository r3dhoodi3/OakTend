# Privacy

This file is retired. It was an early starter note and no longer describes OakTend.

The privacy policy that applies is `src/content/legal/privacy.md`, published at https://oaktend.com/privacy.

What is true today, in short:

- OakTend does not sell personal information and does not share it for advertising.
- A homeowner's name, email, phone and address go to a pro only after the homeowner chooses that pro.
- There is no program that passes homeowner data or "intent" signals to real estate agents. That early idea was dropped and nothing in the product does it.
- OakTend may license de-identified, aggregated statistics about groups of homes, as the published policy describes. Those statistics never include names, addresses or contact details.

Questions: privacy@oaktend.com
